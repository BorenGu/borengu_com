I am a PhD candidate at the [Dahlem Center for Complex Quantum Systems](https://www.physik.fu-berlin.de/en/einrichtungen/ag/ag-eisert/index.html), Freie Universität Berlin, supervised by [Prof. Jens Eisert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jens_Eisert).

I received a First-Class Honours BSc in Mathematical Physics from the University of Edinburgh, supervised by [Dr Latham Boyle](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8d3IJXAAAAAJ&hl=en), followed by an MSc with Distinction at the Higgs Centre for Theoretical Physics as a Higgs Scholar, supervised by [Prof. Chris Heunen](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/cheunen/) and Dr [Joschka Roffe](https://roffe.eu/), and co-supervised by [Dr Robert Booth](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YMsAJG8AAAAJ&hl=en) and [Dr Wang Fang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4D3FWQIAAAAJ&hl=en). During my undergraduate studies, I spent the 2022–23 academic year at UC Berkeley through UCEAP, supervised by [Prof. Nicolai Reshetikhin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolai_Reshetikhin) and co-supervised by [Dr Peng Zhou](https://sites.google.com/view/pzhou/).

My research focuses on **quantum error correction** and **fault-tolerant quantum computation**. I am particularly interested in finite-size quantum LDPC codes, fault-tolerant logical operations, and hardware-aware architectures for scalable quantum computing.
