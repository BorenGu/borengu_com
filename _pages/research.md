My research aims to reduce the physical-resource cost of fault-tolerant quantum computation. I work on quantum codes and logical operations that combine strong error correction with useful computational structure.

## Quantum LDPC codes

Quantum low-density parity-check codes can encode many logical qubits with substantially lower overhead than conventional approaches. I study finite-size code families with competitive parameters and structures that support practical logical operations.

## Fault-tolerant logical operations

Good memories are only one part of a useful quantum computer. I am interested in logical measurements, code surgery, and gate constructions that preserve fault tolerance while allowing operations to be performed efficiently and in parallel.

## Hardware-aware architectures

I also explore how code design and syndrome-extraction circuits can respect realistic hardware constraints, including locality and limited connectivity, without giving up the advantages of high-rate quantum codes.
